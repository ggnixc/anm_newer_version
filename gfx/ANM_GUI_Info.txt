## When making pixelated icons ##
 # Single colour elements with black single pixel lines around it even 'jagged' lines, don't overdo the black.
 # Light (full white) from north and west, dark (full black) from south and east.
 # 25% opacity full black/white block over original colour to get shade/brighter tone from colour below it!.

## GUI General Guidelines ## 
 # Double pixels (in pairs of 2) unless area is small (like the flag frame at startup or the details in the country selection screen)
 # Standard panel: Top bar (blue banner 34px, with 4px gap) height
 # Standard panel: bottom bar 41px (55px with bottons) height
 # Standard panel: AT LEAST 4px frame between outside and inside depth elements, halved when single size px is used
 # Try to keep 1px transparant around all UI elements edging the side of the screen, if possible, for clearity
 # Bright objects should have the 2% opacity ANM LCD filter layer over it to simulate being a 'display within a display'
